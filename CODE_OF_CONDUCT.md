# Community Code of Conduct

FAIA welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds
and experience levels.

Participants are expected to:

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Accept feedback and focus discussion on the work.
- Respect privacy and avoid harassment, insults, or discriminatory conduct.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct is
harmful or disruptive. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
